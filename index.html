OUR STORY

New Capital Roadways

Established around 2000 and owned by Digambar Dia and Brothers, New Capital Roadways is a trusted family-owned packing and moving company providing reliable relocation and transportation services across India.

With years of experience, we are committed to safe handling, careful packing, timely transportation, and dependable customer service. We assist with household and office relocation requirements, making the moving process smooth and hassle-free.

Our services are available 24×7, with customer support through Call and WhatsApp for convenient assistance whenever you need us.

At New Capital Roadways, we believe in serving our customers with professionalism, responsibility, and dedication while building lasting relationships based on trust and reliable service.
